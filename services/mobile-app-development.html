The Decision

Not Every Business Needs a Mobile App.

A mobile app is justified when it creates meaningful value that a normal website cannot provide as effectively.

An app earns its place when

  • Recurring customer interaction
  • Push notifications
  • Location, camera, QR, or device features
  • Offline access
  • Customer accounts and saved activity
  • Subscriptions or digital services
  • Operational field use
  • Staff workflows
  • Marketplace or community behavior
  • Loyalty and repeated transactions
  • A software product sold to users

Consider a web platform instead when

  • Users interact only occasionally
  • The experience is primarily informational
  • Discovery through search is the main need
  • The budget cannot support maintenance
  • No internal owner can support the product
  • The use case is served well by a responsive website or progressive web app

Core Conversion will recommend a web solution when it creates a better commercial outcome than an app.

Before Development

Development Should Begin After the Product Is Defined.

These questions define whether the first release should be a prototype, MVP, production application, or a web platform.

Product BriefTO BE COMPLETED IN DISCOVERY
01 · Business Role
What revenue, operational, customer, retention, or platform objective should the app support?
02 · Primary Users
Who will use it, under what conditions, and how frequently?
03 · Core Job
What recurring problem or task must the product solve well?
04 · Critical Journey
What is the minimum path from opening the app to receiving value?
05 · Business Model
Is the app a paid product, subscription, support channel, operational tool, lead source, marketplace, or companion to another service?
06 · Data and Integrations
What accounts, payment systems, APIs, databases, dashboards, devices, or third-party platforms are required?
07 · Ownership and Operations
Who will approve changes, support users, maintain content, resolve incidents, and fund continued improvement?
