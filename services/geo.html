Definition

Generative Engine Optimization Is the Discipline of Making Information Easier to Use in AI-Assisted Discovery.

Traditional search often presents a list of pages. AI-assisted search may summarize information, compare options, answer questions, and cite or reference selected sources.

No provider controls whether a specific AI system will cite or recommend a business. The goal is to strengthen readiness, clarity, evidence, and digital authority.

GEO improves the conditions that help systems understand

  • 01Who the business is
  • 02What it provides
  • 03Where it operates
  • 04What expertise and evidence it has
  • 05Which questions its content answers
  • 06How facts, entities, services, and sources relate
  • 07Why the information may be credible

Content Standard

What Good AI-Search Content Looks Like.

Content should be useful to people first and structured clearly enough for machines to interpret responsibly. Easy extraction never justifies sacrificing human readability.

Clear question or purpose
Concise direct answer
Sufficient supporting explanation
Named organization or expert
Factual consistency
Visible evidence
Relevant dates
Original examples or data where available
Structured relationships
Useful internal and external references
No unnecessary keyword repetition
No unsupported certainty

Anatomy of an answerable page

The question

A clear heading that states what the page answers

The direct answer

Two or three sentences a system can retrieve — and a person can trust.

Supporting depth

Explanation, original examples, and honest caveats.

Evidence & authorship

Named organization or expert, dates, sources, and proof.

Structured relationships

Internal links, schema, and consistent entities connecting it to the rest of the site.

The limits, stated plainly

  • AI outputs can vary by platform, model, location, account, context, and time.
  • Citation behavior can change.
  • Some systems provide limited reporting.
  • Not all influence can be attributed.
  • Visibility does not guarantee recommendation or conversion.

Fit

GEO Rewards Businesses With Real Substance to Structure.

Strong Fit

  • B2B firms with complex expertise
  • Professional services
  • Clinics and specialized providers
  • Software and technical products
  • E-commerce categories requiring education
  • Companies with strong proprietary knowledge
  • Businesses already investing in SEO and content
  • Brands whose buyers use AI tools to research and compare

A Poor Fit When

  • A weak or inaccessible website
  • No credible information or proof
  • Expectation of guaranteed AI recommendations
  • Desire to mass-produce shallow content
  • Refusal to clarify claims, authorship, or business information
  • No ability to maintain important facts
