The Commercial Problem

A Beautiful Video Can Still Fail to Sell the Idea.

AI can reduce production barriers and expand creative possibilities. It does not replace positioning, message discipline, story structure, brand control, or campaign judgment.

Impressive but unclear

Striking visuals that win attention but leave the audience unsure what is being offered, who it is for, or why it matters — attention without comprehension.

Commercially structured

Commercial creative must help the audience understand:

  • what is being offered
  • who it is for
  • why it matters
  • why the claim should be believed
  • what action should happen next

Core Conversion uses AI as a production capability inside a commercial process — not as the strategy itself.

The Production Method

A Continuous Line From Objective to Delivery.

Stage 01

Commercial Objective

Define the audience, offer, campaign role, desired action, platform, and success criteria.

Stage 02

Positioning and Message

Clarify the problem, promise, proof, difference, objections, and call to action.

Stage 03

Concept and Script

Develop the central creative idea, narrative structure, dialogue or voiceover, visual direction, and duration.

Stage 04

Storyboard and Style Frames

Approve key scenes, composition, product treatment, typography, brand usage, and visual consistency before full production.

Stage 05

AI-Assisted Production

Generate or produce approved scenes, motion, voice, sound, graphics, product imagery, and transitions using the appropriate tools.

Stage 06

Editing and Brand Control

Assemble timing, pacing, audio, subtitles, disclaimers, logo, end card, and campaign message.

Stage 07

Format Adaptation

Prepare approved versions for required placements, aspect ratios, lengths, and file specifications.

Stage 08

Review and Delivery

Apply defined revision rounds, quality checks, rights review, and final export.

Commercial Governance

AI Production, Under Commercial Control.

Every asset moves through a quality-control pipeline before it represents your brand in market.

01

Brand Consistency

Approved product appearance, colors, typography, logo, message, and tone.

02

Visual Continuity

Character, object, environment, lighting, and scene consistency across generated sequences.

03

Claim Accuracy

No unsupported performance, medical, financial, technical, or product claims.

04

Rights and Usage

Tool-license limitations, stock/music terms, voice rights, trademarks, likeness, and client-provided asset permissions.

05

Disclosure and Platform Requirements

Apply disclosures or restrictions required by law, platform policy, sector regulation, or client governance.

06

Human Review

Every final asset is reviewed for narrative clarity, visual artifacts, factual accuracy, brand alignment, and delivery requirements.

AI lowers some production barriers. It does not remove the need for approval, rights management, factual control, or professional review.
